The focus of this study

Main category 3, mineral fuels, lubricants and related materials, is the largest mover in seven of the nine year transitions in the dataset. It sets the direction of the decade.

Subcategory 33, petroleum, petroleum products and related materials, is the focus. It is the only large part of Australian trade where the country buys far more than it sells. Export doubled across the decade and import more than doubled, so the gap between them widened from $16,369m to $38,555m.
